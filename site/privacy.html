Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026. Applies to the Runtrovert app for Android (and its Wear OS watch app) and to this website.

The short version. The Runtrovert app has no server and no account. Your runs, routes, places, notes and settings are saved on your phone. We, the people who make Runtrovert, don't receive them, can't see them and don't sell them. The app fetches three things, each of which you can switch off: map tiles, the weather and the Listen list. A fourth, Things you passed, is off until you switch it on. Your route itself is never sent; with Things you passed on, the squares of about 1 km a run went through are. There are no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no crash reporter that sends anything to us. Apart from the order record Google Play gives us when you buy (see Payments), the only thing we keep about anyone is this website's access log, described at the end.

Who we are

Runtrovert is made by Giuseppe Suelo, an independent developer in Italy, who is responsible for the data described here. For any question about privacy, write to hello@runtrovert.app.

What the app keeps on your phone

To work as a running app, Runtrovert saves the following in a database on your phone. None of it is sent to us.

  • Your runs: the route, distance, time, pace, splits and climb.
  • From your phone's sensors: precise location during a run (also with the screen off, which is why the app asks for background location), and step counts and air pressure to fill gaps in the GPS signal and measure climb.
  • Heart rate, if you use the watch app, recorded into the run.
  • What you tell it: your energy before and after a run, how busy it was, notes, saved places with their names and tags, and your settings.
  • Training plans, if you follow one: your plan and progress, how each session felt, and anything you tell the plan about your health, such as a niggle or pain, days off ill, or a holiday, plus when it last asked how you were. The plan uses these only to ease or rebuild itself on your phone.
  • Runs you bring in from GPX or FIT files you pick.

Reminders are written and scheduled on the phone. There is no push service and nothing outside your phone knows when or whether you ran.

When you choose to open something else

  • Podcasts and shows in Listen open in your own podcast or music app. That app and the show's host see a normal connection, IP address included, like any download. We never see what you play.
  • Sponsors pay a flat fee for a place on the labelled "Kit & offers" shelf. They never learn who opened it or what was tapped. Opening an offer takes you to their website, which has its own privacy terms.
  • Sharing a run goes through your phone's own share sheet, to the app and person you pick. A shared card leaves the route out unless you switch it on.
  • Exporting writes GPX or FIT files to a place you pick. Notes, energy, busyness and anything about your health stay out of those files.

The watch app

The Wear OS app talks to the phone over the direct link between your phone and watch (the Wearable Data Layer that Android provides). Nothing goes through a server of ours, because there isn't one. It reads your heart rate from the watch's sensor only while a run is recording, and sends it to the phone to be kept with that run.

Phone backups

If backups are on in your phone's settings, Android includes Runtrovert's data in your phone's own backup to your Google account, so a new phone can restore it. That copy is kept by Google under your account, not by us. Delete the app and its data is gone from your phone; a phone backup keeps a copy until it's replaced.

Payments

"Everything, once" is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. The app only asks the store whether this store account has bought it. We don't receive your card details. Google gives developers an order record for each purchase, which we use only to handle refunds and taxes.

Permissions

  • Location (precise, and in the background during a run): to record runs.
  • Physical activity: step counts that fill gaps in the GPS signal.
  • Notifications: reminders, and distance and time on the lock screen during a run.
  • On the watch, body sensors (called heart rate on newer Wear OS): heart rate during a run. If you say no, the watch app still shows the run, without heart rate.

Children

Runtrovert is not aimed at children under 13. The app sends no personal data to us from anyone, children included.

Your rights

App data. The app sends us none of your data, so we hold none of it to access, correct or delete. It's all on your phone, where you can delete runs one by one, export them, or delete the app to remove it all.

Website log. If you visited runtrovert.app, its access log may hold your IP address for those visits (see below). You can ask us for a copy or ask us to delete it by writing to us with the IP address and the rough time.

Purchase records. If you bought "Everything, once", you can ask us what the order record from Google Play holds about you.

If you have a question or a complaint, write to us; you can also contact your local data protection authority.

This website

runtrovert.app has no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. It runs on our own server in the Netherlands. Like any web server, it keeps a standard access log of each visit (IP address, time, page requested, the page that linked to it if your browser says, and browser) for security and troubleshooting. This log is about visits to the website only. It's separate from the app and never contains anything from it. We don't use the log for anything else or share it.

Changes

If the app ever fetches something new or keeps something new, this page changes first and the date at the top moves. The Privacy section in the app's Settings says the same thing.

Three dials, all yours

You decide who's there, what you see and how loud it gets.

Most running apps are built to be watched. Runtrovert does what a running app does, and leaves the audience out.

People

Nobody, unless you say so.

  • No feed, followers, kudos or leaderboards
  • Sharing is one run, through your phone's own share sheet
  • Shared routes stay off unless you switch them on

Information

Every number, or none until the end.

  • Clear my head: silent, numbers wait for the finish
  • Just run: distance, time, pace and the map
  • Follow my plan: today's session, block by block

Noise

Quiet by default.

  • No streaks, no "you missed a day", no red badges
  • Reminders written on your phone, never after 9 pm
  • Every reminder has a "Fewer, please" button

Your places

It remembers the loops you love.

Run the same route a few times and Runtrovert offers to keep it. Give it a name, a few tags and a note on how busy it was. It's a private map of the places that feel like yours.

  • Spots the same loop whichever way round you run it
  • "Run this again" puts your old line on the map to follow
  • After a run, mark it Busy, A few people or Hardly anyone
  • "When it's usually quiet here", built only from your own notes Everything, once

Everything you'd expect

A full running app. Nobody watching.

The sky, first

The home screen opens with today's weather in a sentence, the best window to go, what to wear and how long the light lasts.

Every number

Splits, pace and climb for every run, heart rate when a watch is on, and a recording that keeps going with the screen off.

You vs you

Personal bests and a six-week baseline, only ever compared with your own runs. No leaderboards.

Training plans

From your first run to a marathon, built on cautious rules. Week 1 of every plan is free to try.

Listen

Running podcasts that open in your own podcast app. We never see what you play.

Welcome back

Away for ten days or more? It offers an easier way back in, without a guilt trip.

Your files, in and out

Bring runs in as GPX or FIT files, and take them out again for another app or your own records. You can upload them to Strava or Garmin Connect yourself.

On your wrist

A Wear OS watch app with time, distance and pace, Start and Pause, and heart rate into the run, over the direct link between phone and watch.

Eight languages

English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português, Türkçe and 日本語.

What leaves your phone

Runtrovert has no server. Here's the whole list.

Your runs, routes, places, notes and settings are saved on your phone. The app fetches three things, and you can switch each of them off. Like any website, each of these services sees your IP address. Your route itself is never sent.

Map tiles

The street map comes from OpenFreeMap, which sees the area on screen.

Off with Plain map: your route and live dot still work
The weather

Open-Meteo gets a location rounded to about 1 km and sends a forecast back. No key, no account, no cookies.

Off with the weather switch
The Listen list

If you say yes, one file that's the same for everyone, downloaded when you open Listen, plus covers for the shows you scroll past.

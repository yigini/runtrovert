05 Reminders

A nudge. Never a guilt trip.

One or two short notes a day at most, written on your phone. No streaks, no "you missed a day", no red badges, nothing after 9 pm. Every one has a "Fewer, please" button, and "Fewer words" keeps them to the plain fact.

06 Privacy

Nothing of yours on a server. Here's the whole list.

Your runs, routes, places and notes are saved on your phone. The app fetches three things, each with a switch, and a fourth only if you ask for it. The line you ran is never sent.

  • Plain map turns it offMap tilesOpenFreeMap sees which area is on screen.
  • Weather switchThe forecastOpen-Meteo gets a location rounded to about 1 km.
  • Only if you say yesThe Listen listOne file, the same for everyone.

Off until you switch it onThings you passed. After a run, Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap and Wikidata are asked what's in the squares of about 1 km the run went through. They see those squares, which outline roughly where you ran, never the line itself.

Each of these services sees your IP address, like any website. No account, no analytics, no push server. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

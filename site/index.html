No feed, no followers, no kudos, no leaderboards. Nobody asks how it went. If you want to share a run, it's one run, sent to one person, through your phone's own share sheet. The route stays off unless you switch it on.

06Privacy

No server. Here's the whole list.

Your runs, routes, places and notes are saved on your phone. The app fetches three things, each with a switch. Your route itself is never sent.

  • Plain map turns it offMap tilesOpenFreeMap sees which area is on screen.
  • Weather switchThe forecastOpen-Meteo gets a location rounded to about 1 km.
  • Only if you say yesThe Listen listOne file, the same for everyone.

Each of these services sees your IP address, like any website. No account, no analytics, no push server. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
